# Sound Clip Credits

Every clip was shortened, normalised in loudness, converted to mono mp3, and in some cases sped up, pitched up, or given an echo, so each one is an adapted version of its source. The CC BY-SA clips stay under their own licence (CC BY-SA 4.0 or 3.0), which asks anyone sharing them to credit the author, link the licence, say they were changed, and share changed versions under the same licence. The rest of the repository stays under the MIT licence.
